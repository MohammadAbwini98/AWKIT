Related AWKIT features: Recorder navigation/URL tracking, navigation de-duplication, and the distinction between a navigation the user caused and one the page performed on its own.

Why this page exists rather than driving the same transitions from the test: pushState, replaceState and hashchange here are called by this page's own script, exactly as a real SPA router would. A transition injected by page.evaluate runs in a different context and can be captured even when a real application's call is missed, so it cannot prove the wrap actually holds.
